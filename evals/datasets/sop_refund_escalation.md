# Refund and Escalation Handling SOP

Company: Northwind Telecom (fictional). Department: Customer Support. Version 2.1.

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This procedure explains how the support team handles refund requests and escalations for prepaid and postpaid mobile plan customers.

1.2 It applies to all L1 agents, L2 specialists and team leads in the support department.

## 2. Support hours and channels

2.1 Support is available Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00 IST, on phone and chat. Support is closed on Sundays and national holidays.

2.2 Email requests are acknowledged within 24 hours.

## 3. Refund handling

3.1 L1 agents may approve refunds up to INR 5,000 per request without further approval.

3.2 Refunds above INR 5,000 and up to INR 25,000 need approval from a team lead.

3.3 Refunds above INR 25,000 need approval from the finance department.

3.4 Approved refunds are processed promptly and credited to the original payment method.

3.5 Agents must record the refund reason in the CRM before approving.

## 4. Escalations

4.1 If an agent cannot resolve a complaint, the agent escalates it to the relevant manager.

4.2 Escalated complaints must be logged in the CRM with the ticket number and a short summary.

4.3 The customer must be told that the complaint has been escalated.

## 5. Quality and review

5.1 Team leads review 5 percent of closed tickets every week.

5.2 Agents with repeated quality issues receive coaching from their team lead.

5.3 To limit fraud exposure, L1 agents may approve refunds only up to INR 2,500 per request. Higher amounts go to a team lead.

5.4 Customer satisfaction is surveyed after every closed ticket.

## 6. Records and urgent cases

6.1 Chat transcripts and call recordings are stored for a reasonable period and then deleted.

6.2 Urgent complaints received on Sundays are handled by the on-call agent.

## 7. Payments

7.1 Customers can pay by UPI, debit card, credit card or net banking.
